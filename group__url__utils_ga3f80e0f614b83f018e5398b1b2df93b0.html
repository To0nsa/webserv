std::string percentDecodeForm ( const std::string &  input)

Decodes application/x-www-form-urlencoded field content.

First replaces + with a space (per form-url-encoded rules), then applies percent-decoding to HH sequences. This is suitable for decoding both keys and values extracted from a form body.
